## 摘要
分子信息对于成像技术至关重要。光学成像几乎完全通过标记策略获取分子特异性，但这从根本上受到多重成像能力有限、运行成本高和实验复杂性的限制。相反，无标记光学成像在技术上非常简便，但通常被认为缺乏真正的分子特异性。与普遍观点相反，本文介绍了一种无标记的超多重光学成像技术。通过系统研究配对的振动光谱成像和质谱成像，我们发现它们的潜空间表示之间存在惊人的强相关性（超过0.9），并得到了实验和理论的支持。这一发现促使我们构建了监督学习模型，能够直接从各种组织系统的无标记振动图像中成功预测100种分子物种的空间分布。我们开发了这项名为PLANCK（通过先进化学万花筒学习进行预测）的技术，并在器官尺度的红外振动成像和活体组织的拉曼振动成像中进行了演示。在人工智能的驱动下，PLANCK将极其丰富但原本隐藏的振动信息解码为惊人数量（≥100）的特定分子物种，为基础研究和转化应用（包括活体成像）提供了一种具有成本效益且可扩展的解决方案。

## Abstract
Molecular information is vital for imaging technology. Optical imaging acquires molecular specificity almost exclusively via labeling strategy, which is fundamentally constrained by limited multiplexing capacity, high running costs, and experimental complexity. Conversely, label-free optical imaging offers substantial technical simplicity but is believed to have little true molecular specificity. Contrary to common belief, here we introduce super-multiplex optical imaging without labeling. By systematically studying paired vibrational spectroscopic imaging and mass spectrometry imaging, we discovered a surprisingly strong (more than 0.9) correlation between their latent space representations, supported by both experiments and theory. This insight prompts us to build supervised learning models to successfully predict spatial distribution of 100 molecular species directly from label-free vibrational images across diverse tissue systems. We developed this technology, named Prediction through Learning with AdvaNced Chemical Kaleidoscope (PLANCK), and demonstrated it with both infrared-based vibrational imaging of organ-scale tissues and Raman-based vibrational imaging of live tissues. Powered by AI, PLANCK decodes the exquisitely rich but otherwise hidden vibrational information into a surprisingly large number of ( [&ge;] 100) specific molecular species, providing a cost-effective and scalable solution for basic research and translation, including applications in live imaging.
