## 摘要
二维半导体，如单层MoS$_2$，兼具技术上有用的带隙和全表面几何结构，使其作为场效应晶体管沟道和化学敏感器件都具有吸引力。预测性器件设计需要一个输运模型：(i) 基于物理而非经验，(ii) 经过实验验证，(iii) 能够跨越从电子-声子耦合到终端电流和传感器响应的尺度。我们基于ViennaEMC求解器，为单层MoS$_2$提出一个自洽的系综蒙特卡洛（EMC）框架，包含玻尔兹曼输运核心，具有完整的内禀和外禀散射机制（形变势声学声子和谷间声子、极性光学Fröhlich和压电耦合、远程衬底声子、屏蔽的带电杂质和表面粗糙度散射）、简并自由载流子统计以及自洽泊松耦合。内禀输运参数通过与第一性原理密度泛函微扰理论（DFPT）验证，七个量（晶格常数、导带有效质量、带隙、玻恩有效电荷、纵向光学声子能量以及两个面内声速）与参考参数化一致。在多尺度耦合中，EMC速度-场特性曲线输入一维速度饱和沟道模型，该模型捕捉速度饱和场依赖性，并复现了在空气和真空中测量的CVD单层器件的转移特性。最后，环境/吸附物扩展模型再现了测量的对氧分压的电导响应，以及在多个独立器件中测量的NO$_2$和NH$_3$传感的浓度依赖性。

## Abstract
Two-dimensional semiconductors, such as monolayer MoS$_2$, combine a technologically useful band gap with an all-surface geometry, making them attractive both as field-effect-transistor channels and as chemically sensitive devices. Predictive device design requires a transport model that is (i) physically grounded rather than empirical, (ii) validated against experiments, and (iii) able to span the scales from electron-phonon coupling to terminal current and sensor response. We present a self-consistent ensemble Monte Carlo (EMC) framework for monolayer MoS$_2$ built on the ViennaEMC solver, comprising a Boltzmann-transport kernel with a full intrinsic and extrinsic scattering stack (deformation-potential acoustic and intervalley phonons, polar-optical Fröhlich and piezoelectric coupling, remote substrate phonons, screened charged-impurity and surface-roughness scattering), degenerate free-carrier statistics, and a self-consistent Poisson coupling. The intrinsic transport parameters are verified against first-principles density-functional perturbation theory (DFPT), and seven quantities (lattice constant, conduction-band effective mass, band gap, Born effective charges, the longitudinal-optical phonon energy, and both in-plane sound velocities) agree with the reference parametrization. In a multiscale coupling, the EMC velocity-field characteristic feeds a one-dimensional velocity-saturation channel model, which captures the velocity-saturated field dependence and reproduces the measured transfer characteristics of a CVD monolayer device in air and vacuum. Finally, an ambient/adsorbate extension reproduces the measured conductivity response to oxygen partial pressure and the concentration dependence of NO$_2$ and NH$_3$ sensing measured in several independent devices.
