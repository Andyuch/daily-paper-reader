## 摘要
我们展示了在宽带无场自旋电子太赫兹（THz）发射器中对太赫兹发射相位的微秒级电学控制，实现了兆赫兹速率的相位反转，同时克服了传统机械和场驱动方法在千赫兹量级的限制。我们的器件将H型偶极天线与具有单轴磁各向异性的自旋电子异质结构集成，实现了无需外部磁场的确定性自旋轨道转矩诱导的面内磁化切换。通过在H型偶极子的偏置带状线上施加1微秒的电脉冲，在时域信号中直接观察到了相应的太赫兹相位反转。这种无场操作降低了系统复杂性，同时显著扩展了调制带宽。我们的研究结果确立了电可编程自旋电子太赫兹发射器，可用于开发集成片上太赫兹器件和超快应用的紧凑且可扩展平台，包括对太赫兹波形的高速精确控制至关重要的相位敏感光谱和近场成像。

## Abstract
We demonstrate microsecond-timescale electrical control of the terahertz (THz) emission phase in broadband field-free spintronic THz emitters, enabling megahertz-rate phase inversion while overcoming the kilohertz limitations of conventional mechanical and field-driven approaches. Our device integrates an H-dipole antenna with a spintronic heterostructure exhibiting uniaxial magnetic anisotropy, enabling deterministic spin-orbit torque induced in-plane magnetization switching without external magnetic fields. The corresponding THz phase inversion is directly observed in the time domain signal, by applying $1\,μ\mathrm{s}$ electrical pulses on the bias striplines of the H-dipole. This field-free operation reduces system complexity while significantly extending modulation bandwidth. Our results establish electrically programmable spintronic THz emitters that could be used to develop a compact and scalable platform for integrated on-chip THz devices and ultrafast applications, including phase-sensitive spectroscopy and near-field imaging, where high-speed and precise control of THz waveforms is essential.
