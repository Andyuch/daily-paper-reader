## 摘要
宽场中红外光热（MIP）成像能够实现具有亚微米空间分辨率的化学特异性显微成像，但其根本上受限于视野、中红外脉冲能量和光谱调谐能力之间的权衡。因此，目前的宽场实现通常被限制在 200 μm 以下的视野，且仅限于指纹区或高波数光谱区域。在此，我们通过开发一种由商用皮秒光参量振荡器（OPO）驱动的宽场荧光检测中红外光热（F-MIP）成像平台，克服了这些限制。该系统提供高达 360 μJ 的脉冲能量，以及 625 至 4327 cm⁻¹ 的宽调谐范围，从而实现了高波数区域的毫米级宽场成像。我们展示了荧光标记聚苯乙烯微球约 1 mm 直径的视野，同时保持了光谱保真度。此外，据我们所知，该平台实现了首次在 900 cm⁻¹ 以下的宽场 MIP 成像。为了证明其在生物医学成像中的适用性，我们对荧光标记的结核病感染组织切片进行了大面积拼接成像，提供了毫米级样本区域的化学分辨图谱。这些结果确立了宽调谐 OPO 驱动的 F-MIP 作为一个可扩展平台，用于大型生物标本和先进材料的高通量振动成像。

## Abstract
Wide-field mid-infrared photothermal (MIP) imaging enables chemically specific microscopy with submicron spatial resolution but remains fundamentally limited by the trade-off between field of view, mid-infrared pulse energy, and spectral tunability. As a result, current wide-field implementations are typically restricted to fields of view below 200 μm and to either the fingerprint or high-wavenumber spectral regions. Here, we overcome these limitations by developing a wide-field fluorescence-detected mid-infrared photothermal (F-MIP) imaging platform driven by a commercial picosecond optical parametric oscillator (OPO). The system provides pulse energies of up to 360 μJ together with a broad tuning range from 625 to 4327 cm-1, enabling millimeter-scale wide-field imaging in the high-wavenumber regions. We demonstrate a field of view of approximately 1 mm in diameter for fluorescently labeled polystyrene beads while preserving spectral fidelity. Furthermore, the platform enables, to our knowledge, the first wide-field MIP imaging below 900 cm-1. To demonstrate its applicability to biomedical imaging, we performed large-area mosaic imaging of fluorescent tuberculosis-infected tissue sections, providing chemically resolved maps over millimeter-sized sample areas. These results establish broadly tunable OPO-driven F-MIP as a scalable platform for high-throughput vibrational imaging of large biological specimens and advanced materials.
