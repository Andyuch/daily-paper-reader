## 摘要
飞秒激光烧蚀在飞秒到毫秒的时间尺度上，将光沉积的能量重新分配到电子、结构、机械和热自由度中。然而，这些耦合过程通常在不同的时间范围内通过不同的可观测量进行测量，限制了早期瞬态动力学、残余热效应和最终形貌之间的定量比较。本文介绍了一种泵浦-探测全息成像技术，该技术在匹配的成像条件下，能够重建整个时间范围内的振幅和相位分辨光场。将该方法应用于BK7玻璃的深紫外飞秒烧蚀，捕捉了从早期激发和去除阶段动力学到残余基底加热和永久改性的转变过程。差分相位分析分离了瞬态烧蚀层的亚纳秒演化过程以及材料去除后的微秒残余热效应。在烧蚀阈值以上，坑深随通量增加而增大，而残余热信号饱和，表明额外吸收的能量优先分配给材料去除和烧蚀相关过程，而非保留为基底热量。这些结果揭示了通量依赖的能量分配是低热影响飞秒加工的动力学基础，并确立了全息成像作为追踪激光驱动非平衡材料转化的一种途径。

## Abstract
Femtosecond laser ablation redistributes optically deposited energy across electronic, structural, mechanical, and thermal degrees of freedom over timescales from femtoseconds to milliseconds. However, these coupled processes are usually measured in separate temporal ranges and through different observables, limiting quantitative comparison between early transient dynamics, residual heating, and final morphology. Here we introduce pump-probe holographic imaging that reconstructs amplitude- and phase-resolved optical fields across this full temporal range under matched imaging conditions. Applied to deep-ultraviolet femtosecond ablation of BK7 glass, the method captures the transition from early excitation and removal-stage dynamics to residual substrate heating and permanent modification. Differential phase analysis isolates sub-nanosecond evolution of the transient ablating layer and microsecond residual heating after material removal. Above the ablation threshold, crater depth increases with fluence, whereas the residual thermal signal saturates, indicating that additional absorbed energy is preferentially partitioned into material removal and ablation-related processes rather than retained as substrate heat. These results identify fluence-dependent energy partitioning as a dynamical basis of low-heat-affected femtosecond processing and establish holographic imaging as a route to tracking laser-driven nonequilibrium material transformations.
