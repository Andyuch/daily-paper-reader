## 摘要
微波能够相干地访问低能激发，并作为量子与磁性系统中高频自旋动力学的高效探针。对于拓扑自旋织构，微波激发预计会产生丰富的集体响应，然而直接实空间观测超快动力学仍存在局限。此处，我们利用时间分辨洛伦兹透射电子显微镜，可视化由中心反斯格明子与表面斯格明子组成的混合反斯格明子结构中的微波驱动动力学。我们解析了反斯格明子面积与二次谐波信号的皮秒级演化，为微波激发下自旋织构的非线性响应提供了证据。我们追踪了反斯格明子与表面斯格明子的核心运动，二者遵循不同轨迹，但共享相同的旋转方向。微磁模拟复现了关键观察结果，并将动态模式与核心轮廓沿厚度的空间调制联系起来。这些成果确立了超快电子显微镜作为一种强大的实空间方法，用于探测拓扑磁孤子的高频微波驱动动力学。

## Abstract
Microwaves provide coherent access to low-energy excitations and serve as effective probes of high-frequency spin dynamics in quantum and magnetic systems. For topological spin textures, microwave excitation is expected to generate rich collective responses, yet direct real-space observation of ultrafast dynamics remains limited. Here we use time-resolved Lorentz transmission electron microscopy to visualize microwave-driven dynamics in a hybrid antiskyrmion structure composed of a central antiskyrmion and surface skyrmions. We resolve the picosecond evolution of antiskyrmion area and second-harmonic signals, evidencing nonlinear responses of spin textures under microwave excitations. We track the core motions of the antiskyrmion and surface skyrmions, which follow distinct trajectories while sharing the same rotational sense. Micromagnetic simulations reproduce the key observations and associate the dynamic modes with the spatial modulation of the core profile along the thickness. These achievements establish ultrafast electron microscopy as a powerful real-space approach for probing high-frequency microwave-driven dynamics of topological magnetic solitons.
