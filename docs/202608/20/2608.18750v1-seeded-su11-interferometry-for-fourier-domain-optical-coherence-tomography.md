## 摘要
我们展示了一种基于种子SU(1,1)干涉仪的傅里叶域光学相干层析（FD-OCT）。多层物体使用中心波长为1550nm的宽带光进行探测，而深度分辨的3D图像则从中心波长为810nm的光子通量测量中重建。我们表明，在低参量增益区，种子注入增加了光子通量，使得能够使用光谱仪而非单光子探测器进行体积成像。我们的分析进一步表明，在所考虑的条件下，与增加参量增益相比，种子注入提供了一条更有效的灵敏度增强途径。

## Abstract
We demonstrate Fourier-domain optical coherence tomography (FD-OCT) based on a seeded SU(1,1) interferometer. Multilayer objects are probed with broadband light centered at 1550nm, while depth-resolved 3D images are reconstructed from photon flux measurements centered at 810nm. We show that, in the low parametric-gain regime, seeding increases the photon flux, enabling volumetric imaging with a spectrometer rather than single-photon detectors. Our analysis further shows that, under the conditions considered, seeding provides a more effective route to sensitivity enhancement than increasing the parametric gain.
