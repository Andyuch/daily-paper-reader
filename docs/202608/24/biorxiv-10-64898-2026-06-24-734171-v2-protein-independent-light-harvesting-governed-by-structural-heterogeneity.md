## 摘要
氯小体是已知最大的光合光捕获天线，但与基于蛋白质的天线不同，它们缺乏组织色素分子的蛋白质支架，而是包含自组装的管状和层状细菌叶绿素聚集体。这些天线如何实现定向且高效的能量转移至今仍未解决。通过将超快瞬态吸收光谱应用于单个野生型和突变型氯小体，我们解析了被系综平均掩盖的六个动力学组分，并将每个组分归属于与层状或管状聚集体相关的激发过程。层状聚集体倾向于激子局域化并扩展光捕获能力，而管状聚集体保持相对离域的激子特性，并作为基板的主要能量供体。因此，结构异质性作为一种功能特征出现，平衡了光捕获能力与稳健的定向能量转移。这些发现揭示了自组装色素聚集体在没有蛋白质支架的情况下实现高效光捕获的劳动分工。

## Abstract
Chlorosomes are the largest known photosynthetic light-harvesting antennas, yet unlike protein-based antennas, they lack protein scaffolds that organize pigment molecules and instead contain self-assembled tubular and lamellar bacteriochlorophyll aggregates. How these antennas achieve directional and efficient energy transfer has remained unresolved. By applying ultrafast transient absorption spectroscopy to individual wild-type and mutant chlorosomes, we resolved six kinetic components obscured by ensemble averaging and assigned each to an excitation process associated with either lamellar or tubular aggregates. Lamellar aggregates tend toward exciton localization and expand light-harvesting capacity, whereas tubular aggregates retain a relatively delocalized excitonic character and serve as the primary energy donors to the baseplate. Structural heterogeneity therefore emerges as a functional feature that balances light-harvesting capacity with robust directional energy transfer. These findings reveal a division of labor among self-assembled pigment aggregates for efficient light harvesting without protein scaffolds.
