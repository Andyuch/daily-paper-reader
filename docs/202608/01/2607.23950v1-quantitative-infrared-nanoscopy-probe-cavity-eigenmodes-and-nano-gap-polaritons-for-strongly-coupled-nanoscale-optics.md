## 摘要
光学纳米显微术（包括近场光学显微与光谱）凭借尖锐照射探针尖端产生的纳米尺度光聚焦，绕过了传统光学的衍射极限。然而，尽管尖端与其介电环境之间的强光学耦合既增强了纳米显微测量灵敏度，又可能在纳米间隙腔中产生超强场，但该耦合的条件在现有解析模型中仍未得到充分量化。在此，我们提出了一种稳健的探针-腔本征模式形式体系，完整描述了探针与环境之间的相互近场相互作用如何产生一种对外部场的复合响应，该响应在定性上不同于其各个组分的响应。这一“EigenProbe”模型将实际光学纳米显微术的基本激发识别为纳米间隙极化激元，为准确预测近场显微与光谱实验提供了优雅的基础，尤其是在探针-样品相互作用非微扰的情况下。通过与极性声子和分子振动的精细受控纳米显微实验对比，我们展示了纳米间隙极化激元如何被实现并用于可靠且快速“反演”局域光学常数。这一进展既需要对探针响应进行定量校准的细致理解，也需要我们对探针尖端腔本征模式散射的高效半解析描述。我们的EigenProbe形式体系为将多样化且不断扩展的光学纳米显微术成熟为纳米尺度光学环境的精密计量学奠定了基础，并指导未来利用纳米间隙腔操纵量子材料的局域激发以及实现光子发射体上的强耦合。

## Abstract
Optical nanoscopies including near-field optical microscopy and spectroscopy circumvent the diffraction limit of conventional optics thanks to the nanoscale light focus emerging at the apex of a sharp irradiated probe. However, while strong optical coupling between the apex and its dielectric environment affords both enhanced nanoscopic measurement sensitivity and potentially ultra-strong fields within a nano-gap cavity, the conditions for this coupling remain poorly quantified by prevailing analytic models. Here we present a robust formalism of probe-cavity eigenmodes that fully describes how mutual near-field interactions between probe and environment produce a composite response to external fields qualitatively distinct from that of its distinct components. This "EigenProbe" model identifies the fundamental excitations of realistic optical nanoscopies as nano-gap polaritons, which provide an elegant basis to accurately predict near-field microscopy and spectroscopy experiments especially when probe-sample interactions are non-perturbative. Through comparison to carefully controlled nanoscopies of polar phonons and molecular vibrations alike, we show how nano-gap polaritons are both realized and utilized for reliable and rapid "inversion" of local optical constants. This advance demands both a careful understanding of the probe response through quantitative calibration, and our efficient semi-analytic description of cavity eigenmode scattering at the probe apex. Our EigenProbe formalism sets the stage for maturing diverse and proliferating optical nanoscopies into precision metrologies of nano-scale optical environments, and guides future use of nano-gap cavities to manipulate local excitations of quantum materials and to achieve strong coupling over photonic emitters.
