## 摘要
在亚角秒尺度上实现高角分辨率成像，从根本上受限于光传播介质和光路中光学元件引起的波前像差。因此，在这些精细角尺度上准确恢复源结构，依赖于对波前像差的实时精确传感和校正。受射电干涉测量的启发，我们开发了使用掩模孔径的不同方法，可直接测量电磁波前畸变的振幅和相位，同时重建源结构。第一种方法是射电干涉测量风格的自校准，能够恢复孔径上的复电场像差，相位精度达亚角秒，相当于纳米级的光程精度，同时重建的源结构精度达毫角秒（亚微米）级。第二种方法基于闭合不变量的源重建，完全绕开自校准及其误差，同时获得相当的源结构保真度。这些方法已在ALBA同步辐射光束线上得到验证。这两种方法共同为纳米级高精度波前传感和亚角秒级高角分辨率成像提供了可靠框架，为掩模孔径干涉测量开辟了新的可能性。潜在应用涵盖实验室和同步辐射设施乃至天文学领域，包括大型强子对撞机中的束流诊断以及詹姆斯·韦布空间望远镜等空间望远镜上的掩模孔径干涉测量。

## Abstract
Achieving high-angular-resolution imaging on subarcsecond scales is fundamentally limited by wavefront aberrations imparted by the propagation medium and by optical elements along the light path. Accurate recovery of source structure at these fine angular scales therefore relies on precise, real-time sensing and correction of wavefront aberrations. Drawing inspiration from radio interferometry, we have developed different approaches using masked apertures that directly measure both the amplitude and phase of the distortions to the electromagnetic wavefront while simultaneously reconstructing the underlying source structure. First is radio interferometry style self-calibration which can recover the complex electric field aberrations across the aperture with subarcsecond phase accuracy, equivalent to nanometer-level precision in optical pathlength, and simultaneously reconstruct the source structure with milliarcsecond (sub-micron) accuracy. Second is closure invariant-based source reconstruction which allows to bypass self-calibration and errors therein entirely while achieving comparable fidelity in the recovered source structure. These methods have been validated on the ALBA synchrotron beamline. Together, these methods provide a reliable framework for nanometre-scale high-precision wavefront sensing and subarcsecond-scale high angular resolution imaging, enabling new possibilities for masked-aperture interferometry. Potential applications span laboratory and synchrotron facilities to astronomy, including beam diagnostics in the Large Hadron Collider and masked-aperture interferometry on space telescopes such as the James Webb Space Telescope.
