## 摘要
纳米尺度系统中的光热相互作用已成为一种多功能工具，可用于选择性热沉积和光学响应的稳健调谐，其应用范围从热疗到光信号的切换与路由等众多领域。然而，迄今为止，关于瞬态光热相互作用的全面理论仍然缺失。当光激发持续时间与热输运相关的时间尺度相当时，此类理论的发展尤其具有挑战性，这一新兴领域呈现出光吸收与热致光学响应变化之间相互关联的瞬态相互作用。在此，我们发展了一种耦合模理论，能够捕捉各种纳米尺度系统中复杂的瞬态光热现象。我们进一步揭示了实现最优能量沉积和加热的条件。作为示例场景，我们将模型应用于设计具有高对比度、高效温度与光学切换的超表面，展示了快速冷却至静止状态（40纳秒内）的能力。除了对瞬态温度分布和光学响应的精细控制外，我们的理论还为纳米尺度光热相互作用提供了深刻的物理见解，可在从医疗到有源光子学和制造等多个领域得到应用。

## Abstract
Photothermal interaction in nanoscale systems has emerged as a versatile tool for selective heat deposition and robust tuning of optical responses with a myriad of applications from hyperthermal medical treatment to switching and routing of optical signals. However, to date a comprehensive theory of transient photothermal interaction is missing. Development of such a theory is particularly challenged when optical excitation duration is comparable to the timescale related of heat transport, an emergent regime with a mutually interconnected transient interplay of light abortion and heat-induced change of optical responses. Here, we develop a coupled mode theory that captures intricate transient photothermal phenomena in a wide range of nanoscale systems. We further reveal conditions for optimal energy deposition and heating. As an example scenario we apply our model to design metasurfaces with high contrast and efficient temperature and optical switching, demonstrating fast cooling to the rest state (within 40 ns). Beyond exquisite control of both transient temperature profiles and optical responses, our theory offers deep physical insights onto photo-thermal interaction at the nanoscale which can find use in variety of fields from medical treatment to active photonics and manufacturing.
