## 摘要
我们通过一种在真实位置附近初始化的期望最大化算法实现了GML估计器，并通过仿真验证了该理论。

## Abstract
We realize the GML estimator by an expectation-maximization algorithm initialized near the true positions and verify the theory by simulation.
