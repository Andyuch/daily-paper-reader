## 摘要
叠层衍射成像（Ptychography）是一种强大的无透镜成像技术，能够接近衍射极限，但其性能越来越受到非理想探测硬件的制约。在光子受限的测量中，微弱的高频衍射信号常常与空间上不均匀的探测器噪声重叠，而大多数重建算法仍将探测器视为理想测量平面。在这里，我们将探测器信息引入的测量一致性纳入叠层衍射重建中。通过校准逐像素传感器响应，该方法构建了空间分辨的置信图，并将其嵌入迭代振幅约束中，从而在保留具有物理意义的衍射信息的同时，对不可靠的探测器残差进行降权。在透射、反射和弱生物相位成像中的实验表明，衍射数据质量得到改善，信噪比（SNR）提升约两倍，重建接近瑞利极限，测得的（k）因子约为0.65。与先前先进的去噪方法相比，所提出的框架在抑制探测器引起的背景与保留结构衍射信息之间实现了更好的平衡。这些结果表明，探测器可靠性可用作环路内的物理约束，以扩展使用不完美传感器的叠层衍射成像的性能。

## Abstract
Ptychography is a powerful lensless imaging technique capable of approaching the diffraction limit, yet its performance is increasingly constrained by non-ideal detection hardware. In photon-limited measurements, weak high-frequency diffraction signals often overlap with spatially heterogeneous detector noise, whereas most reconstruction algorithms still treat the detector as an ideal measurement plane. Here, we introduce detector-informed measurement consistency into ptychographic reconstruction. By calibrating the pixelwise sensor response, the method construct a spatially resolved confidence map and embed it into the iterative amplitude constraint, allowing unreliable detector residuals to be down-weighted while preserving physically meaningful diffraction information. Experiments across transmission, reflection, and weak biological phase imaging show improved diffraction-data quality, an approximately twofold signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) enhancement, and reconstruction approaching the Rayleigh limit with a measured (k)-factor of about 0.65. Compared with previous advanced denoising methods, the proposed framework achieves a better balance between suppressing detector-induced background and preserving structural diffraction information. These results show that detector reliability can be used as an in-loop physical constraint to extend the performance of ptychographic imaging with imperfect sensors.
