## 摘要
二维材料中的原子尺度波纹可以改变界面电子耦合，但其对超快载流子弛豫的影响仍不清楚。在此，我们利用结构表征和时间分辨双光子光电子能谱比较了单层h-BN/Ir(111)和h-BN/Pt(111)界面的镜像势态（IPS）。与文献一致，h-BN在Ir(111)上具有强波纹，而在Pt(111)上相对平坦。第一（n = 1）和第二（n = 2）IPS在两个基底上出现相似的能量，但其弛豫动力学显著不同。在h-BN/Ir(111)上，IPS衰减受响应限制（<20 fs），而h-BN/Pt(111)则表现出56 fs（n = 1）和75 fs（n = 2）的寿命。寿命差异最一致的解释是波纹增强IPS波函数与金属基底的重叠，从而加速电子衰减。这些结果表明，原子尺度波纹是调控二维材料与金属界面超快电子动力学的有效物理参数。

## Abstract
Atomic-scale corrugation in two-dimensional materials can modify interfacial electronic coupling, yet its influence on ultrafast carrier relaxation remains poorly established. Here, we compare image potential states (IPS) at monolayer h-BN/Ir(111) and h-BN/Pt(111) interfaces using structural characterization and time-resolved two-photon photoemission spectroscopy. Consistent with literature, h-BN is strongly corrugated on Ir(111) but comparatively flat on Pt(111). The first (n = 1) and second (n = 2) IPS appears at similar energies on both substrates, whereas their relaxation dynamics differ markedly. On h-BN/Ir(111), the IPS decay is response-limited (<20 fs), while h-BN/Pt(111) exhibits lifetimes of 56 fs (n = 1) and 75 fs (n = 2). The lifetime contrast is most consistently explained by corrugation-enhanced overlap of the IPS wavefunction with the metal substrate, which accelerates electron decay. These results indicate that atomic-scale corrugation is an effective physical parameter for tuning ultrafast electron dynamics at two-dimensional material and metal interfaces.

### 2. 方法论：核心思想、关键技术细节
- **核心思想**：利用IPS作为界面电子动力学的敏感探针——其波函数延伸至真空但与金属基底有重叠，寿命受表面势垒和电子耦合的强烈影响。通过对比不同波纹度的h-BN/金属体系，直接建立结构与超快弛豫之间的关联。

- **对比体系**：单层h-BN/Ir(111)与h-BN/Pt(111)，两者表面投影电子结构相似（Ir和Pt），但h-BN波纹度差异显著（Ir强，Pt弱），构成“等静态结构、异动态”的理想对比。

### 6. 主要结论与发现
- h-BN/Ir(111)与h-BN/Pt(111)的IPS能级、色散和静态电子结构几乎一致，但IPS寿命差异显著：Ir上n=1寿命<20 fs（响应受限），Pt上n=1为56±23 fs、n=2为75±19 fs。
