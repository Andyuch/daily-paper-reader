## 摘要
对于具有全介电常数和磁导率张量以及任意取向光轴的周期性光子结构，我们提出了据我们所知首个支持自动微分和高效GPU加速的各向异性严格耦合波分析框架。该方法将层内传播表述为边值问题，并利用精细积分方法直接构造散射矩阵，从而避免了大型非厄米矩阵的特征分解。在精度相当的情况下，所提方法比基于特征分解的方法实现了25倍的加速。其与自动微分的兼容性进一步展示了在各项异性光子结构拓扑优化方面的强大潜力。该框架为复杂各向异性器件的正向分析和逆向设计提供了一种高效的计算工具。

## Abstract
For periodic photonic structures with full permittivity and permeability tensors and arbitrarily oriented optical axes, we present, to the best of our knowledge, the first anisotropic rigorous coupled-wave analysis framework that supports automatic differentiation and efficient GPU acceleration. The method formulates intralayer propagation as a boundary-value problem and directly constructs the scattering matrix using the precise integration method, thereby avoiding eigendecomposition of large non-Hermitian matrices. At comparable accuracy, the proposed method achieves a 25-fold speedup over the eigendecomposition-based approach. Its compatibility with automatic differentiation further demonstrates strong potential for topology optimization of anisotropic photonic structures. This framework provides an efficient computational tool for both forward analysis and inverse design of complex anisotropic devices.
