## 摘要
准布拉格区是大动量转移原子干涉测量的一个良好折中方案，它既能扩大干涉面积，又能限制不需要的态布居。然而，当使用激光冷却原子而非具有亚反冲速度分布的超冷原子时，通过标准飞行时间方法分离动量态可能具有挑战性。为了克服这一限制，我们使用拉曼光谱在 interrogating 激光脉冲期间对动量空间中的原子态演化进行频闪采样。我们定量表征了利用双光子（2ħk）和多光子（6ħk）布拉格跃迁的原子光学，后者可选地通过最优控制协议增强。我们使原子态的观测动力学与模拟紧密匹配。最后，我们在一个6ħk布拉格重力仪中对输出态进行了动量光谱分析。

## Abstract
Quasi-Bragg regime is a good compromise for large-momentum-transfer atom interferometry, allowing for scaling up the interferometric area, while constraining the population of unwanted states. Separation of momentum states via standard time of flight methods, however, can be challenging when using laser-cooled atoms, rather than ultracold atoms with sub-recoil velocity distribution. To overcome this limit, we use Raman spectroscopy for stroboscopic sampling of the atomic state evolution in momentum space during the interrogating laser pulses. We quantitatively characterize atom optics employing two-photon (2___k) and multi-photon (6___k) Bragg transitions, the latter being optionally enhanced with optimal control protocol. We closely match the observed dynamics of the atomic state with simulations. Finally, we perform momentum spectroscopy of the output states in a 6___k Bragg gravimeter.
