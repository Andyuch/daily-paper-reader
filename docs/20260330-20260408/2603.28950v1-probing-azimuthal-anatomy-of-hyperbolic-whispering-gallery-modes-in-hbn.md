## 摘要
散射型扫描近场光学显微术 (s-SNOM) 是研究极化激元模式的强大工具。然而，该技术的一个固有局限性在于激发和探测发生在同一位置。这一限制使得解析具有复杂空间结构的激发变得具有挑战性，因为这些激发可能对入耦合条件表现出微妙的依赖性。在此，我们提出了一种通过引入辅助腔作为固定近场激发源来克服这一限制的策略。这种配置允许 s-SNOM 探针仅充当探测器，从而实现了激发与探测的解耦。我们将该方法应用于六方氮化硼谐振器中双曲声子极化激元的回音壁模式 (WGMs)。通过空间分辨的近场图谱，我们直接观察到了具有大且离散角向动量（k_φ/ k_0 高达 15）的亚波长极化激元 WGMs。这使我们能够绘制模式的频率和角度行为。值得注意的是，我们观察到 WGMs 对有效折射率的动态调节，以在不同激发条件下保持一致的角向动量 k_φ。数值模拟支持了实验观察结果，并证实了双曲 WGMs 的存在。该方法实现了双曲腔中此前隐藏的模式结构的直接可视化，并为动量控制的极化激元器件工程开辟了新途径。

## Abstract
Scattering-type scanning near-field optical microscopy (s-SNOM) is a powerful tool for investigating polaritonic modes. However, an inherent limitation of this technique is that excitation and detection occur at the same location. This constraint makes it challenging to resolve excitations with complex spatial structures, which can exhibit delicate dependence on the in-coupling conditions. Here, we present a strategy to overcome this limitation by introducing an auxiliary cavity, which serves as a stationary near-field excitation source. This configuration allows the s-SNOM tip to act solely as a detector, and decouples excitation from detection. We apply this approach to whispering gallery modes (WGMs) of hyperbolic phonon-polaritons in hexagonal boron nitride resonators. Through spatially resolved near-field maps we directly observe subwavelength polaritonic WGMs with large and discrete azimuthal momentum ($k_φ/ k_0$ up to 15). This allows us to map the frequency and angular behavior of the modes. Notably, we observe dynamic tuning of the effective refractive index by the WGMs to preserve consistent azimuthal momentum \(k_φ\) under varying excitation conditions. Numerical simulations support the experimental observations and confirm the observation of hyperbolic WGMs. This approach enables direct visualization of previously hidden mode structures in hyperbolic cavities and opens new pathways for momentum-controlled polaritonic device engineering.
