## 摘要
胶原结构的定量无标记分析缺乏经过物理校准的体积方法。在此，我们展示了全息断层扫描（HT），它可以重建胶原网络的三维折射率（RI）分布。由于折射率与干质量密度呈线性相关，HT 仅需单次采集即可获得纤维级形态和质量。HT 能够解析三维原纤维结构，区分特定亚型的组织和质量差异，并追踪聚合过程和细胞驱动的重塑，为细胞外基质分析建立了一个定量框架。

## Abstract
Quantitative label-free analysis of collagen architecture lacks physically calibrated volumetric methods. Here we present holotomography (HT), which reconstructs three-dimensional refractive-index (RI) distributions of collagen networks. Because RI scales linearly with dry mass density, HT yields fiber-level morphology and mass from a single acquisition. HT resolves 3D fibrillar structure, distinguishes subtype-specific organizational and mass differences, and tracks polymerization and cell-driven remodeling, establishing a quantitative framework for extracellular matrix analysis.
